The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) is India’s national space agency, established in 1969 and headquartered in Bengaluru. It operates under the Department of Space, Government of India.

Key points:

Mission: Develop space technology and apply it for national development (communication, weather forecasting, navigation, disaster management, and scientific research).

Launch vehicles: Notable rockets include the PSLV (Polar Satellite Launch Vehicle) and GSLV (Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle).

Major achievements:

Chandrayaan-1 discovered water molecules on the Moon.

Mars Orbiter Mission (Mangalyaan) made India the first Asian nation to reach Mars orbit.

Chandrayaan-3 achieved a successful soft landing near the Moon’s south pole in 2023.

Applications: Runs satellite systems for TV broadcasting, internet, GPS-like navigation (NavIC), remote sensing, and meteorology.

In short: ISRO is a globally respected, cost-efficient space agency that designs rockets, launches satellites, and explores space while supporting everyday services in India.
